Help & Setup Guide

How to Connect

  1. Sign in or create your account above.
  2. Scroll up to the Connect Your WhatsApp panel.
  3. Enter your phone number with country code (e.g. 254712345678).
  4. Click Get Code — an 8-character code appears.
  5. Open WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link a Device → tap Link with phone number.
  6. Enter the code. Your bot connects within seconds ✅
  1. Sign in or create your account above.
  2. Scroll up to the Connect Your WhatsApp panel.
  3. Click Generate QR Code — a live QR code pops up.
  4. Open WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link a Device.
  5. Point your phone at the QR code. Done ✅
  6. Note: QR codes expire in ~60 seconds — act fast.
